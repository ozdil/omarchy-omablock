# Privacy Policy for OmaBlock & OmaBlock Shield Bridge

Last Updated: October 1, 2026

OmaBlock and the OmaBlock Shield Bridge extension are committed to protecting your privacy.

## 1. Information Collection
OmaBlock and OmaBlock Shield Bridge do NOT collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII), browsing history, search queries, IP addresses, or web traffic.

## 2. Local Processing
All ad-blocking, tracker prevention, and cosmetic DOM filtering actions are performed strictly on your local device. The extension operates completely offline and self-contained.

## 3. Third-Party Sharing
We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any user data with any third parties, advertisers, or analytics platforms under any circumstances.

## 4. Permissions Usage
- `declarativeNetRequest`: Used exclusively to block known tracking and advertising network requests.
- `storage`: Used solely to save local user toggle settings on the device.
- `nativeMessaging`: Used strictly to communicate with the local host daemon on Omarchy Linux without network exposure.
- `host_permissions` (`<all_urls>`): Used strictly to inject cosmetic CSS rules that remove empty ad placeholders on visited pages.
